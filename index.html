Verification methods

  • Daily production logs. Flour, yeast, salt and fuel matched to bundle output over 31 days.
  • Field spot-checks. Bundle weight and quality (10 properly baked loaves) and hygiene during loading, offloading and distribution.
  • Receipt audit. Signed receipts cross-checked with camp coordinators.
  • Oral consultations. Face-to-face, with no paperwork burden on beneficiaries.

Key findings

  • Quality and need. High satisfaction with freshness and texture. Cooking gas and firewood are scarce and unaffordable, so bread is the main staple.
  • Timing. Early delivery matters, so bakery shifts start at 6:00 AM, before peak movement and heat.
  • Targeting. Dense camps with newly displaced families get extra scheduled rounds.

Challenges and mitigation

ChallengeImpact on operationsMitigation taken
Fuel supply fluctuations. Inconsistent supply and high prices for generators and ovens.Potential interruption to daily production schedules.Emergency fuel reserves on site and priority delivery schedules agreed with vendors.
Temporary bakery structure. Sheet-metal arches, public rest facilities and tarpaulins.Exposure to weather and heat build-up, limited space, harder sanitation control.Daily structural inspections, stronger sanitation protocols, shaded ventilated storage and cooling areas.
Heat exposure and worker fatigue. Long hours beside ovens under tarpaulins.Exhaustion, heat stress, lower efficiency, safety hazards.Rest rotations, hydration and cooling breaks, heavy baking moved to early morning.

Lessons learned

  • Keep a fuel buffer. On-site reserves, emergency supplier agreements and early reorder points.
  • Protect workers from heat. Rotations, cooling breaks and early-morning baking.
  • Maintain temporary sites. Constant upkeep, organized space and strict hygiene.
  • Work through camp committees. Trusted leaders verify lists and manage queues.

Planned for the next period

  • Continuous operation. Hold production at 900+ bundles per operating day.
  • Maintenance overhaul. Preventive maintenance on mixers, ovens and generators.
  • Supply chain. Pre-position flour, yeast and fuel for September.
  • Beneficiary expansion. Re-assess camp populations in Al-Mawasi and Khan Younis for new shelter points.
